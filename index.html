Privacy Policy

AirTag, Mic, Camera Detector · Effective date: October 24, 2026

AirTag, Mic, Camera Detector (the “App”) is an Android app provided by Opixx (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what information the App collects, how we use it, and who we share it with. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Location data

The App requests access to your device’s location. Location access is needed so the App’s scanning and detection features can work, for example when scanning for nearby Bluetooth devices. You can allow or deny this permission at any time in your Android settings. If you deny it, some detection features may not work.

Device information

The App and the third-party services it uses collect technical information about your device, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, crash logs, diagnostics, and app-instance identifiers.

Advertising ID

The App collects your device’s Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID, “GAID”) so that ads can be served, measured, and limited in frequency. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Purchase and subscription information

If you subscribe, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of your subscription status (for example, whether it is active), but we do not receive or store your payment card details.

What we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or phone number through the App. The App does not record, store, or upload audio, video, or photos. Detection features run on your device.
